Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want their TV to work — not to sell them things.

Instant navigation

Zero-lag app scrolling on a TV remote, powered by a custom recycling shelf built from scratch.

Home row + app drawer

A favourites row on the home screen and a pull-down drawer for everything else. Reorder freely with the D-pad.

Button shortcuts

Assign your remote's colour and menu keys to any app — right from the home screen, no Accessibility Service.

Zero telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no background network calls. What you do on your TV stays on your TV.

Manage apps

Long-press any app to move, hide, open App info, or uninstall. Hidden apps stay installed — just off your home screen.

Wallpaper support

Set any wallpaper, rendered with hardware bitmaps that save 8–32 MB of RAM versus software rendering.

3-state clock

Full time + day/date, time-only, or off. Locale-aware, 12/24-hour, and updates instantly on timezone changes.

Wi-Fi & settings pills

Quick Wi-Fi access with a live connection indicator, plus a gear pill — long-press it to jump straight to system settings.

Cold-start cache

Wallpaper, shelf, and icons all paint in the very first frame on every launch after the first.

Under the hood

Tiny by design.

  • Zero runtime dependencies — no AppCompat, Material, RecyclerView, ConstraintLayout, or Lifecycle.
  • Fully programmatic UI — no XML layouts, no inflated resource bloat. Everything drawn in code.
  • Custom recycling shelf instead of RecyclerView — tight control, zero overhead.
  • R8 + resource shrinking on every release build. Dead code has nowhere to hide.
  • Pure Java — no Kotlin standard library shipped.
  • No native code, no lib/ folder — one universal APK runs on every architecture.
