Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for people who want their TV to work — not to sell them things. Every feature earns its place; nothing runs in the background.

Instant navigation

Zero-lag scrolling on a TV remote, via a custom recycling shelf built from scratch — no RecyclerView.

Home row + app drawer

A favourites row up front and a pull-down drawer for the rest. Reorder freely with the D-pad.

Button shortcuts

Assign your remote's colour and menu keys to any app — right from the home screen, no Accessibility Service.

Manage apps

Long-press any app to move, hide, open App info, or uninstall. Hidden apps stay installed, just off your home screen.

Wallpaper support

Any wallpaper, rendered with hardware bitmaps that save 8–32 MB of RAM versus software rendering.

3-state clock

Full time + day/date, time-only, or off. Locale-aware, 12/24-hour, instant on timezone changes.

Wi-Fi & settings pills

Live Wi-Fi indicator, plus a gear pill — long-press it to open system settings instantly.

Zero telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no background calls. What you do on your TV stays on your TV.

~140KB

One universal APK. Runs on every Android architecture, with zero runtime dependencies.

Under the hood

Tiny by design.

  • Zero runtime dependencies — no AppCompat, Material, RecyclerView, ConstraintLayout, or Lifecycle.
  • Fully programmatic UI — no XML layouts, no inflated resource bloat.
  • Custom recycling shelf instead of RecyclerView — tight control, zero overhead.
  • R8 + resource shrinking on every release build.
  • Pure Java — no Kotlin standard library shipped.
  • No native code, no lib/ folder — same APK on ARMv7, ARMv8 and x86_64.
